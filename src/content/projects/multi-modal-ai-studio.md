Voice AI Workbench (formerly Multi-modal AI Studio) connects independent ASR, LLM or VLM, and TTS services. Start with browser audio and Speaches + Gemma, compare recorded session timelines, then configure server-local USB audio for robot prototypes. Optional vision and NVIDIA speech backends remain available.
