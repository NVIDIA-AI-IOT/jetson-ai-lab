This intermediate, 20+ hour curriculum takes developers through an end-to-end Physical AI workflow with the Seeed reBot Arm B601-RS and the NVIDIA robotics stack.

Learners collect demonstrations with Hugging Face LeRobot, build a digital twin in NVIDIA Isaac Sim, augment scenes with Cosmos Transfer, fine-tune an Isaac GR00T VLA model, and deploy it for real-time inference on Jetson.

### Topics Covered

- Robot teleoperation and data collection with LeRobot
- Digital twin simulation with NVIDIA Isaac Sim
- Scene augmentation with NVIDIA Cosmos Transfer
- VLA model fine-tuning with NVIDIA Isaac GR00T
- Real-time edge deployment on Jetson Thor
