Use [`microduck-cli`](https://github.com/agentculture/microduck-cli) to provision and operate a simulated MicroDuck, including stand-up, head motion, skills, rules, and reinforcement-learning workflows. Any agent or human drives the robot through the same CLI.

The project documents validated paths for Jetson AGX Thor and Jetson AGX Orin, along with hardware-specific training limitations.
